Ultima Adventures House Gallery

Every prebuilt house the placement tool offers, rendered from the client's own art files. Each house has a floor selector: a floor view hides everything above that floor, the same way the game does when you stand inside. Prices are the bank cost at placement.

Storage and lockdowns are the house's secure-container and item limits. Footprint is the multi's tile extent, including steps. Floors are detected from the walkable tiles in the multi, so a roof terrace or a steeple can count as a floor. Doors and the house sign are added by the server at placement and are not part of the artwork.
